Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide security.

2. Cookies We Use

Our website only uses cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work securely. Because of this, we do not need to ask for your consent before setting them.

Strictly necessary cookies

  • csrftoken – a security cookie that protects our enquiry form from forged submissions. It lasts up to one year.
  • messages – briefly passes a confirmation message from one page to the next (for example, after a form is sent). It is deleted straight away.
  • sessionid – used only when our own staff sign in to manage the website. Visitors do not receive it.

Your privacy pop-up choice

When you close our privacy pop-up, your browser stores a small setting (sp_cookie_notice_ok) on your own device so the pop-up does not appear on every visit. It is not sent to us.

3. Analytics and Advertising

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not track you across other websites. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

4. Managing Cookies

You can restrict or block cookies through your web browser settings. Your browser's Help pages explain how. Disabling essential cookies may prevent form submissions or restrict access to the administration area.
