Cookie Policy

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide security.

2. Cookies We Use

Our B2B catalogue website uses a minimal set of cookies, divided into:

Essential Cookies

These are required for core website functionality, such as Django session cookies for admin access and CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery) protection tokens for secure form submissions. The site cannot function properly without these.

Preferences

We store a single local storage setting (cookie_consent_accepted) to remember that you accepted our cookies banner, preventing the banner from showing on subsequent visits.

3. Analytical Tracking

To understand visitors' behavior, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is privacy-first, cookieless, and does not collect or track any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not load third-party marketing cookies or track you across other websites.

4. Managing Cookies

You can restrict or block cookies through your web browser settings. Please consult your browser's Help menu for specific instructions. Disabling essential cookies may prevent form submissions or restrict access to the administration area.
