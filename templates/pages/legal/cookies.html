1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide security.

2. Cookies We Use

{% if ga_measurement_id %}Our website uses cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work securely, which do not need your consent, and Google Analytics cookies only if you agree to them.{% else %}Our website only uses cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work securely. Because of this, we do not need to ask for your consent before setting them.{% endif %}

Strictly necessary cookies

  • csrftoken – a security cookie that protects our enquiry form from forged submissions. It lasts up to one year.
  • messages – briefly passes a confirmation message from one page to the next (for example, after a form is sent). It is deleted straight away.
  • sessionid – used only when our own staff sign in to manage the website. Visitors do not receive it.

Your cookie choice

Your choice in our privacy pop-up is stored on your own device (dr_cookie_consent) so the pop-up does not appear on every visit. It is not sent to us.

3. Analytics Cookies (only with your consent)

If you press Accept All, or switch on Analytics in Customise, we use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, to count visits and see which pages are useful, so we can improve our website. Google Analytics is not loaded and these cookies are not set unless you agree.

  • _ga – tells visits from different browsers apart. It lasts up to 2 years.
  • _ga_<ID> – remembers the current visit. It lasts up to 2 years.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not use Analytics to identify you. You can change your choice at any time with (also at the bottom of every page). Switching Analytics off deletes its cookies from this website.

{% else %}

Your privacy pop-up choice

When you close our privacy pop-up, your browser stores a small setting (sp_cookie_notice_ok) on your own device so the pop-up does not appear on every visit. It is not sent to us.

3. Analytics and Advertising

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not track you across other websites. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

4. Managing Cookies

You can restrict or block cookies through your web browser settings. Your browser's Help pages explain how. Disabling essential cookies may prevent form submissions or restrict access to the administration area.
