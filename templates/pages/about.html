Our Roots

Spice Pantry is the trading brand of Desi Roots, a dedicated food and grocery wholesaler based in Bristol, United Kingdom. We specialize in supplying commercial kitchens, restaurants, caterers, supermarkets, and grocery shops with top-quality dry goods.

Our core selection includes six major product lines: Rice, Spices, Flour, Oils, Lentils, and Pickles. By sourcing products directly and managing our own local storage and distribution fleet, we ensure competitive pricing, maximum product freshness, and reliable delivery service.

Serving Bristol & Nearby Areas

We deliver directly with our own logistics fleet to restaurants and food service providers across Bristol, Bath, and nearby locations. Our local approach means we can offer flexible delivery slots, personal customer care, and quick response times that larger national distributors cannot match.
