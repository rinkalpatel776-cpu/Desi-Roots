About Desi Roots

Desi Roots is a Bristol-based wholesale supplier of Indian and Asian spices and groceries. We supply restaurants, takeaways, caterers and independent shops across Bristol and the surrounding areas.

Our story

Desi Roots was founded by a couple who started working in hospitality over a decade ago. We began in entry-level roles and worked our way up to management, learning first-hand which ingredients Asian kitchens depend on. That taught us what a kitchen needs from its supplier: quality ingredients, fair prices, stock that is there when you need it, and service you can rely on.

We started Desi Roots to be the kind of wholesale supplier we would have wanted: one that understands the businesses it serves.

Our products

We supply a wide range of wholesale products for Indian, South Asian, Asian and Far Eastern cooking, to restaurants, takeaways, caterers, shops and other food businesses.

Our product range includes:

  • Authentic Indian and Asian spices, herbs and masalas.
  • Rice, lentils, pulses and grains.
  • Flours, atta and essential cooking ingredients.
  • Frozen foods, snacks and appetisers.
  • Sauces, pickles, chutneys and condiments.
  • Canned, bottled and packaged grocery essentials.
  • Far Eastern and international food ingredients.

We source from trusted brands and suppliers, so you can order a wide variety of products in one place.
