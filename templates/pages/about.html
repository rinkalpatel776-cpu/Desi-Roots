Welcome to Desi Roots – Your Trusted Wholesale Partner for Authentic World Foods

At Desi Roots, we are a Bristol-based wholesale supplier of high-quality Asian and Chinese ethnic spices and grocery products. Serving restaurants, takeaways, caterers, independent retailers, and households across Bristol and the surrounding areas, we are passionate about bringing the authentic and diverse flavours of India, Asia and Far East to your kitchen.

Our Story – Built from Experience, Driven by Passion

Desi Roots was founded by a passionate couple whose journey in the hospitality industry began over a decade ago. Starting from entry-level roles and working our way up to managerial positions, we gained invaluable hands-on experience and developed a deep appreciation for the unique ingredients that define Asian cuisine. Having experienced the industry from the ground up, we know the importance of quality ingredients, competitive prices, consistent availability and dependable service.

This real-world experience inspired us to establish Desi Roots with one clear vision: to become a wholesale partner that genuinely understands and supports the businesses we serve.

Our Products – Bringing the World to Your Business

We offer an extensive range of wholesale products covering Indian, South Asian, Asian and Far Eastern cuisines, catering to the diverse needs of restaurants, takeaways, caterers, retailers and other food businesses.

Our product range includes:

  • Authentic Indian and Asian spices, herbs and masalas.
  • Rice, lentils, pulses and grains.
  • Flours, atta and essential cooking ingredients.
  • Fresh and frozen vegetables.
  • Frozen foods, snacks and appetisers.
  • Sauces, pickles, chutneys and condiments.
  • Canned, bottled and packaged grocery essentials.
  • Far Eastern and international food ingredients.

We work hard to source quality products from trusted brands and suppliers, giving our customers access to a wide variety of products under one roof.
